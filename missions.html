Teaching a class? Build a SoC on the Build page, then pick a bug here. You get a link that opens your SoC on the Verify page with that bug hidden. Students run tests and make their diagnosis. The bug is only encoded in the link, not encrypted, so it stops guessing, not determined peeking.
